Use this as the official OneMil brand manual for all future design and UI work.

OneMil is a premium competition app for CZ/SK users. The brand must feel modern, trustworthy, luxurious and energetic. It must never look like casino, gambling, betting, lottery or cheap “get rich quick” design.

Final visual direction: dark premium tech-luxury.
Typography: Poppins for headings, Inter for body/UI text.
Main accent: orange (#FF8A00).
Base colors: black, dark navy, graphite.
Premium accents: metallic silver, platinum and subtle champagne/gold.

Primary logo: OneMil wordmark with trophy/number 1 behind or connected to text.
Secondary logo: trophy/number 1 above OneMil wordmark.
Icon-only: trophy/number 1 symbol.

Do not use casino, hazard, betting, jackpot, chips, roulette, cards or slot-machine aesthetics.
Do not change business logic, routes, database, payments, wallet, contests, tickets, winners or Partner Offers unless explicitly requested.
When applying this manual, only adjust visual styling, typography, colors, spacing, logo usage and brand consistency.
